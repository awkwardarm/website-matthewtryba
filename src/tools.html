Production Tools

Free tools and utilities I've built to speed up music production in Ableton Live and Apple Logic Pro.

TRYBA Strip for Ableton Live

Strip the silence out of audio clips in Ableton Live's Arrangement View, like Strip Silence in Pro Tools or Logic. Set a threshold on one clip, then strip your whole session one track at a time, with every cut landing on the beat grid so clips are easy to move later.

*Requires Ableton Live 12 or higher, with Live Suite or the Max for Live add-on

Transpose All for Ableton Live

Transpose all midi tracks in Ableton with one knob.

*Requires Ableton Live 12 or higher, with Live Suite or the Max for Live add-on

Stem Logic for Logic Pro

Automate exporting all individual tracks solo'd through the master in Logic Pro.
