Privacy Policy

Effective October 20, 2026

This policy explains what information TRYBA MUSIC, LLC ("we", "us") collects through matthewtryba.com and the tools we run, how we use it, and the choices you have. We keep it simple: we collect only what we need to work with you, and we never sell your information.

Information you give us

  • Contact forms. When you send a message, we receive your name, email address, any links you share and your message.
  • Free tools sign-up. When you request the free producer tools, we receive your name and email address so we can email you the download link.
  • Donations. Donations are handled by Stripe on Stripe's own pages. We never see or store your card details.

Form submissions are emailed to us and saved in a private Google Sheet that only we can open. Download emails are sent through our email provider, Resend.

Information collected automatically

This site uses Google Analytics and Google Ads to understand how visitors find and use the site and to measure our advertising. These services use cookies and similar technology to collect things like the pages you visit, your browser and device type, and the ad you clicked to get here. If you arrive from one of our ads, the form you submit may include the ad's click ID so we can tell which ads work. You can opt out of Google Analytics with the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on and manage ad personalization in your Google ad settings.

The site is hosted on Cloudflare, which processes basic request data (such as IP addresses) to deliver and protect the site. Pages that embed YouTube videos load content from YouTube, which is covered by Google's privacy policy.

How we use your information

  • To reply to you and work with you on your music.
  • To send you the tools or information you asked for.
  • To understand and improve the site and our advertising.

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal information.

Our YouTube publishing tool

We use an internal tool to upload and schedule videos to our own YouTube channel and to read the view, like and comment counts of those videos. It uses YouTube API Services. The tool is used only by us, on our own Google account. It does not access anyone else's YouTube account or data.

  • By using our YouTube channel, you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service.
  • YouTube API Services are provided by Google. See the Google Privacy Policy.
  • The tool stores only our own video IDs, titles, descriptions, release times and video statistics, on our own computers. None of it is shared with anyone else.
  • Access granted to the tool can be revoked at any time on the Google security settings page.
  • Our use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

How long we keep it

We keep contact and sign-up information for as long as it's useful for working with you, or until you ask us to delete it.

Your choices

You can ask us what information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. To make a request, send us a message through the contact form.

Children

This site isn't directed to children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update it on this page and change the date at the top.

Contact

TRYBA MUSIC, LLC. Questions about this policy? Reach us through the contact form.
