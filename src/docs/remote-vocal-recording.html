How It Works

1 We connect over Zoom Desktop on your computer, with your microphone plugged in on your end.
2 I take remote control of your computer and dial in the recording: levels, routing, and monitoring all handled for you.
3 I set it up so you hear yourself in perfect time with the music, no distracting delay in your headphones.
4 From there, you focus only on the performance. I capture every take and take care of all the technical.
A Note on the Tech
Built Into Your First Song

The first time we record together, we do a one-time Remote Studio Setup (about two hours) run by me over Zoom — where I dial in your entire rig. There's no separate charge for it: the cost is folded into your first remote song, so you only ever pay it once. After that, getting ready for each session takes only a quick 10–15 minute self-setup using your Remote Vocal Session Tech Setup Guide. This setup is dialed in for your current computer. If you switch machines or your system changes significantly down the road, a quick re-setup gets you running again.

If your gear hasn't arrived or your computer isn't ready when we start, we'll simply reschedule the setup rather than lose our session time.

On Your End

Apple Silicon Mac

An M1 or newer Mac with 16 GB of RAM, ~20% storage free, kept up to date. On a PC? Check with me first.

Stable Internet

A solid, reliable connection. A wired Ethernet connection is ideal; strong Wi-Fi works too.

Wired Headphones

Closed-back and wired. Never use Bluetooth, it adds delay that makes recording very difficult.

A Quiet Space

A private room where you won't be interrupted, that has little to no background noise or echo.
