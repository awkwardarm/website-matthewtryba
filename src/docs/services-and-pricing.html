Ways to Work Together

A clear path from our first session to a finished record. Start wherever feels right. Each step is designed to build naturally into the next and there's no pressure to go further than you want.

Start small with confidence — one session on a song credits toward its Gold Tier Demo or Full Production within 90 days.

01
Creative Date $400one-time

A focused working session to dig into one song together, see if we click creatively, and leave with a rough capture of what we make. It runs in person or remote with as little tech as possible. Offered once, to new clients only.

02

Lead vocal plus a light instrumental bed and a rough mix — the bones of a real production you can feel.

03

A fully realized demo — polished vocals, a richer instrumental arrangement, and a more finished mix.

04
The Destination
Full Production from $4,000

The complete, release-ready record: produced, performed, and mixed to a professional, major-label standard.

Recording Vocals Remotely

These premiums apply only when you track your vocals from your own space, over video. Dialing in your rig, troubleshooting live, and singing takes a little slower without being in the room together. It's priced per song, on top of each offering below. Your very first remote song runs higher, a once-ever amount that folds in getting your home rig fully dialed. Every remote song after is the standard rate.

Advanced Demo +$575 first song, then +$225/song
Gold Tier Demo +$700 first song, then +$350/song
Full Production +$950 first song, then +$600/song

Recording your vocals in person? None of this applies, even when the writing, arrangement, and production happened remotely. Coming to me to track vocals is simply an in-person session at the base rate.

The Creative Date is exempt either way. It's a low tech, "get to know each other" session that works in person or remote at no extra cost.

The Fine Print

Credit applies once per song. Your most recent Creative Date or Advanced Demo on a song credits toward a Gold Tier Demo or Full Production of that same song within 90 days; a Gold Tier Demo credits toward Full Production. Each song gets one credit. once it's applied, any later step on that song is at full price. The Creative Date and Advanced Demo don't credit toward one another. Credits can't be combined, banked, or moved between songs.

Credit is based on the base price of the offering. Remote vocal premiums aren't creditable.

Remote vocal recording is billed per song on top of the base offering; recording vocals in person carries no premium, even when the writing and production happened remotely. The higher first-song rate applies just once to your first remote vocal session across any offering and folds in the one-time home-rig setup.

All prices are flat per the scope above. When you're ready, we'll pick the right starting point together.
