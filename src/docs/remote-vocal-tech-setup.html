Before Every Session 10–15 min

Your Quick Tech Setup Guide

Once your one-time studio setup is done, run this quick check before each session.

Your rig is already dialed in. This pre-flight takes about 10–15 minutes and makes sure we can spend all our time together on the music.

The Gear You'll Need

1
Your computer, with the Zoom Desktop app installed. Your recording software is already set up — I installed and configured it during our one-time setup, and I'll open it from my end. Nothing for you to launch.
2
Your microphone setup. Either a USB microphone, or an audio interface with a separate XLR microphone.
3
Wired headphones. This is the most important part. Please don't use Bluetooth or wireless headphones — they create a delay that makes recording very difficult.

Before our session: please don't apply chapstick or other lip products that cause mouth noises.

For your one-time setup: I'll be installing your recording software during the setup, so have your computer's password handy.

The 15-Minute Setup

Please follow the instructions for the type of setup you have.

Option 1 For artists using a USB microphone
Step 1 — Plug Everything In
  • Connect your USB mic to your computer.
  • Plug your wired headphones directly into the headphone jack on the microphone itself, not into your computer.
Step 2 — Check Your Mic Level
  • On the microphone, find the "Gain" knob (not the headphone knob).
  • Speak at a normal volume and turn the knob up until you're about 75% of the way to the maximum. We don't want it peaking into the red.
Option 2 For artists using an audio interface
Step 1 — Plug Everything In
  • Connect your microphone to your audio interface using an XLR cable.
  • Connect your audio interface to your computer (usually with a USB cable).
  • Plug your wired headphones directly into the headphone jack on the audio interface, not into your computer.
Step 2 — Check Your Mic Level
  • On the microphone, find the "Gain" knob (not the headphone knob).
  • Speak at a normal volume and turn the knob up until you're about 75% of the way to the maximum. We don't want it peaking into the red.
Step 3 Set Up Zoom — for everyone
Open the Zoom app before our call.
Go to Zoom Workplace › Settings › Audio.
Set Speaker to whatever your headphones are physically plugged into — your audio interface, or your USB microphone.
Set Microphone to your computer's built-in microphone. This is just so we can talk. Your real microphone goes straight into the recording, not into Zoom — that's what keeps it sounding like a record.
Leave everything else on this page alone.
Step 4 Give Zoom Permission to Share — for everyone
So I can set up and run the recording from my end, macOS needs to let Zoom share your screen and accept my control. This usually only has to be done once, but a system update can quietly turn it off.
Go to System Settings › Privacy & Security.
Turn Zoom ON under Screen & System Audio Recording.
Turn Zoom ON under Accessibility.
If macOS asks you to quit and reopen Zoom, do that now — not once we're already on the call.

That's it. Looking forward to making music.
