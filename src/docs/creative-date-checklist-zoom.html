Creative Date Checklist

Now that our Creative Date is booked, here's how to prep before we hop on Zoom.

I'm excited for our session. Here's a quick checklist to prep. Bring your ideas, I'll handle the rest.

What to Bring

1
Your Song Ideas
  • Voice memos
  • Lyrics
  • Rough demos  (if you have them)
2
Reference Tracks (2–3 songs, if applicable)

Pick a few tracks that give us a ballpark idea of the direction you have in mind. Don't go too crazy finding the perfect reference, we're just looking for a general vibe.

3
Practical Items
  • Your computer, with Zoom Desktop installed  (a laptop or desktop works much better than a phone or tablet)
  • Wired headphones  (preferred over Bluetooth)

Tech Setup

Connection A stable internet connection is key for a smooth session.
Be Ready to Sing We'll record a "scratch vocal" as a rough guide for the production. It doesn't need to be perfect — just warm up your voice a bit beforehand if you can.

Looking forward to creating together.
